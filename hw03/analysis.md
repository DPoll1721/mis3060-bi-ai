# HW3 Analysis — Executive Changes vs. Earnings Timing

Based on my data, executive changes mostly precede earnings announcements:
22 of 28 events (79%) were filed before the nearest earnings release, 6 came
after, and 2 (Walmart's CEO transition from Doug McMillon to John Furner) fell
within the same week. The pattern varies by company — every JPMorgan and Walmart
event came before earnings, Apple and NVIDIA were mixed, and Microsoft leaned
after (2 of 3). Counting by filing instead of by person (16 filings) gives the
same picture: 11 before, 4 after, 1 same week. One possible explanation is that
companies prefer to announce leadership changes ahead of earnings so the call can
address them, but with only five companies and earnings roughly every 90 days,
this is a small sample and the "nearest earnings" label can flip on a few days'
difference.
